# Executive Summary Generator

## Purpose
Read a Medibank data table and produce a polished executive summary for senior management, board papers, or regulatory submissions.

## Before you begin
- Confirm the purpose of the report
- Confirm the audience such as board, APRA, or management team
- Confirm the time period covered

## Workflow

### Step 1 - Read the data
Scan all visible data. Identify key metrics, time period, highest values, lowest values, and notable changes.

### Step 2 - Identify top insights
Select the most notable positive result, the most notable area of concern, and the most significant trend.

### Step 3 - Confirm data points
List 5 specific data points to use. Ask for confirmation before writing.

### Step 4 - Write the summary
Write 4 paragraphs: Context, Key Findings with specific numbers, Areas of Attention with specific figures, Outlook. Total 150 to 200 words, professional corporate tone.

### Step 5 - Add Key Metrics panel
Add a 4 row table: top performer, lowest performer, biggest improvement with percentage, biggest decline with percentage.

### Step 6 - Format and place
Place summary below the data with a visible border. Label as Executive Summary with the time period.

## Output
- Executive summary below the data
- Key Metrics panel table
- 150 to 200 words, professional tone

## Common pitfalls
- Every number must come from the visible data
- Do not exceed 200 words
- Do not use first person pronouns
- Confirm 5 data points before writing
