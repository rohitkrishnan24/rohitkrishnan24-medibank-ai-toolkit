# Workforce Productivity Tracker

## Purpose
Track and analyse Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption and time savings across Medibank teams to demonstrate ROI, identify low adoption areas, and build the business case for continued AI investment.

## Before you begin
- Confirm the sheet contains: Team Name, Department, Active Copilot Users, Total Staff, Weekly Sessions, Avg Minutes Saved Per Session, Primary Use Case
- Confirm the reporting period

## Workflow

### Step 1 - Adoption rate by team
Calculate Adoption Rate as Active Copilot Users divided by Total Staff times 100. Flag teams below 30 percent as Low Adoption. Flag above 70 percent as High Adoption.

### Step 2 - Time saved calculation
Calculate Weekly Time Saved as Weekly Sessions times Avg Minutes Saved Per Session. Convert to hours. Calculate Annual Time Saved as Weekly Time Saved times 48 working weeks.

### Step 3 - Financial value of time saved
Estimate financial value as Annual Hours Saved times average hourly cost of 65 dollars. Display as total dollar value of productivity gain per team.

### Step 4 - Analyse by department
Group by Department. Calculate total adoption rate, total weekly sessions, total annual hours saved, and total financial value. Rank departments by financial value descending.

### Step 5 - Use case analysis
Group by Primary Use Case. Calculate number of teams using each use case and average time saved per session. Identify highest value use cases.

### Step 6 - Productivity dashboard
Create new sheet Productivity Tracker with: organisation wide summary, team adoption league table, department breakdown, use case analysis, and total ROI estimate.

## Output
- New Productivity Tracker sheet
- Low adoption teams highlighted
- Total ROI estimate calculated
- Department and use case rankings

## Common pitfalls
- Adoption rate counts active users not licences assigned
- Time saved figures are self-reported estimates — treat as indicative not precise
- Adjust the 65 dollar hourly rate if actual Medibank rates differ significantly
