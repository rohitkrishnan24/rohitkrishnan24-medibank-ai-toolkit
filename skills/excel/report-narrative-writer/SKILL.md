# Report Narrative Writer

## Purpose
Transform a structured data table into a polished written report narrative using specific numbers, directional language, and professional corporate tone.

## Before you begin
- Confirm the purpose of the report
- Confirm the audience: board, management team, regulator, or internal team
- Confirm the time period covered
- Confirm the most important metric

## Workflow

### Step 1 - Read the data
Identify highest value, lowest value, average, biggest change, and notable outliers in the active data range.

### Step 2 - Determine structure
Management report: headline then detail then outlook. Board paper: strategic implication then evidence then recommendation. Internal briefing: operational summary then exceptions then next steps.

### Step 3 - Write the narrative
Section 1 as one sentence headline with the most important finding and specific number. Section 2 as 3 to 4 sentences covering top 2 positives and top 1 concern with specific figures. Section 3 as 1 to 2 sentences on what the trend suggests for next period.

### Step 4 - Format and place
Place narrative below the data table with a visible border. Label as Report Narrative with the time period.

### Step 5 - Confirm accuracy
List the 5 data points used and ask for confirmation before finalising.

## Output
- Narrative placed below data with clear label and border
- 150 to 250 words
- Professional report ready tone

## Common pitfalls
- Every claim must reference a specific number from the data
- Do not exceed 250 words
- Do not use first person pronouns
- Confirm 5 data points before writing
